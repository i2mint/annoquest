Pilot plan

Goals · Timeline · Budget

Goals

The pilot tests whether field teams can capture a reading in under two minutes with the new handheld kit, without a technician present.

Success means at least 90% of readings pass the automatic quality check in the first week.

Timeline

We will ship the pilot to three sites in two weeks, starting on the first Monday after sign-off.

What could slip

The kit vendor has not confirmed delivery dates. If the kits arrive late, the pilot moves by the same amount; we will not shorten it.

Budget

The budget is “fixed” at 10k — no more. Any overrun is cut from the third site, not added.

Open questions

Who signs off the quality threshold? Today nobody owns it.
